# styles

Application-wide Sass foundations. Keep reusable tokens and primitives here; component-specific styles should remain beside their component.

- `core/`: resets, typography, colors, and animation primitives.
- `components/`: global styles for shared visual patterns.
- `themes/`: theme definitions and variable overrides.
- `utils/`: mixins, functions, and utility classes.
- `index.scss`: the public entry point for the style system.
